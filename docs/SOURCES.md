# Evidence and limits
Local requirements reviewed: TAF-J26-IT-405.pdf (four component tasks); IT23205710 (1).pdf (recognition proposal); image.png (panel notice). The panel and individual report disagree on re-ranking ownership; see COMPONENT_OWNERSHIP.md.

Primary external sources checked 13 October 2026:
- Keras recurrent layers: https://keras.io/api/layers/recurrent_layers/
- Keras temporal Conv1D: https://keras.io/api/layers/convolution_layers/convolution1d/
- Abhishek & Sumanathilaka (2025), End-to-End Sign Language Recognition Pipeline: Towards Energy Efficient Modeling. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2025.07.208 ; author research summary: https://sumanathilaka.github.io/research.html (the previously supplied IIT page was unavailable during this review)
- Google MediaPipe Hand Landmarker: https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/hand_landmarker (video/live image input; detector is not a sign-language translator).
- Microsoft MS-ASL project: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/ms-asl/ (illustrates unseen-signer evaluation; ASL vocabulary is not evidence of SLSL word mappings).
- Madushani (2025), A phonological study of minimal pairs in Sri Lankan sign language, DOI 10.64920/IPRC25047. Available abstract describes phonological contrasts; no verified named word pairs were obtained for this recording plan.

The vocabulary, clip counts, model sizes and schedule here are practical design proposals, not quantities prescribed by these papers. No named SLSL minimal pair or multi-meaning mapping is asserted without expert validation. No measured performance is supplied.
